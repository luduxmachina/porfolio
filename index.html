Barkitos Animals

Join Barkito, a friendly robot, on an imaginary adventure to rescue toy animals. Explore an archipelago full of challenges, take care of each toy, and complete rescue quotas. A charming game that combines exploration, light management, and strategy, with a style inspired by toys and a world full of imagination.

Genre: Adventure / Simulation
Engine: Unity
Platforms: PC Web
Status: In development
